## EVM and decoding definitions
softEvm is RMS symbol error normalized by true-symbol energy. Effective SINR is 10*log10(1/mean(softEvm^2)); packet-level dB values are not arithmetically averaged.
In the summary, codewordPassRate is the success fraction over decoded codewords. In paired binary comparisons, codewordPass means that every codeword in the payload passed. The one- and three-codeword conditions must not be treated as identical packet-pass trials.

## Limitations
